Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delivery, food safety, and ordering.

1. Is there a minimum order value for delivery?

No! There is no strict minimum order limit. Whether you want a single Kulhad Pizza or a full party combo, we happily accept your order. Delivery is 100% FREE in Udaipur!

2. How long does delivery take in Udaipur?

Average delivery time is 25 to 35 minutes depending on your exact locality in Udaipur. Because we bake our Kulhad Pizzas fresh inside clay kilns, baking takes about 8-12 minutes, followed immediately by express dispatched delivery.

3. Are all dishes 100% Vegetarian?

Yes, absolutely. Dipjit's Pharma Kitchen is a 100% pure vegetarian cloud kitchen. We do not handle any meat, poultry, or egg products in our facility. We use 100% pure dairy mozzarella and fresh cottage cheese (paneer).

Yes! While we do not offer dine-in seating (cloud kitchen format), you are welcome to place a self-takeaway order and pick it up hot from our counter in Udaipur.
