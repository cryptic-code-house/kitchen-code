Meet the Founder

Prescribing Pure Flavor & Uncompromising Cleanliness

Dipjit Ghosh started his journey not in a traditional commercial culinary school, but surrounded by pharmaceutical beakers, strict sterilization protocols, and a deep appreciation for bio-chemical hygiene.

As a student living in Udaipur, Tripura, he frequently observed how most street food joints sacrificed basic sanitization and ingredient purity in pursuit of speed. Cravings for cheesy pizzas or crunchy fried momos were often followed by upset stomachs and health worries.

Dipjit asked a simple question: "Why can't fast food be prepared with the sterile precision of a pharmaceutical lab and the warm, soul-soothing soul of artisan cooking?" That question sparked Dipjit's Pharma Kitchen.

"We prefer hygiene, taste, quality and users experience over anything."

Clinical Protocols

Our 6 Hospital-Grade Kitchen Standards

How we ensure every parcel leaving our cloud kitchen is sterile, fresh, and 100% pure vegetarian.

🧴

1. Medical-Grade Stainless Steel

All prep surfaces are food-grade SS304 stainless steel, disinfected with food-safe sanitizers between every single order batch.

🧤

2. Double-Barrier PPE Prep

Our chefs always wear sterile hairnets, latex-free food prep gloves, and chef coats. Zero bare-hand food contact.

🪴

3. Traditional Clay Baking

Our Signature Kulhad Pizzas are baked inside virgin terracotta earthenware, eliminating toxic non-stick chemical coatings.

💧

4. Triple-Stage RO UV Water

All dough kneading, beverage mixes, and vegetable washing uses multi-stage RO + UV purified mineral water.

🌱

5. 100% Pure Vegetarian Assurance

Strictly vegetarian premise. Fresh milk paneer, premium mozzarella, farm vegetables, and zero animal additives or gelatin.

📦

6. Tamper-Evident Thermal Packaging

Every order is heat-sealed with a security sticker. If the seal is broken, your meal is replaced instantly free of cost.
