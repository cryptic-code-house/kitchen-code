Behind the Prescription

"We prefer hygiene, taste, quality and user experience over anything."

Dipjit’s Pharma Kitchen was born out of a real mission in Udaipur, Tripura. As a pharmacy student with an unyielding love for culinary art, our founder Dipjit Ghosh realized people shouldn't have to compromise on health or cleanliness when craving indulgent street favorites.

Here, every sauce is crafted fresh without harsh artificial chemicals, every pizza is baked in sterile clay ovens, and every delivery box is double-sealed for hospital-standard safety.
